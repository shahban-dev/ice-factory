Definitions

Billed = sum of sale totals by sale date (opening balances NOT billed). Collected = sum of payments by payment date (incl. toward opening balances). Expenses = sum of expenses by expense date incl. salary payments. Sales profit = Billed − Expenses. Cash profit = Collected − Expenses. Customer balance = opening_balance + sale totals − payments (positive = owes, negative = advance credit).
